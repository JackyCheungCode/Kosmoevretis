# Kosmoevretis Design System

- IBM Carbon Design System v11 is the visual foundation; default theme is Gray 10.
- Use Carbon semantic tokens for color, typography, spacing, status, and interaction.
- Establish hierarchy through layer backgrounds and spacing. Reserve borders for meaningful boundaries, dense data separation, form affordances, and interaction states.
- Avoid decorative shadows and unnecessary card outlines.
- Every intelligence metric must disclose unit, period, geography, and evidence type.
- Distinguish observed facts, third-party estimates, and AI inference.
- Follow Carbon grid breakpoints: 320, 672, 1056, 1312, and 1584px.
- Keyboard access, visible focus, semantic landmarks, and reduced-motion preferences are required.
